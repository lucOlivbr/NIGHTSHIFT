skateboard_kenney.glb is the real, unmodified model including its embedded
colormap texture. Runtime scale is 1.2. See skate_manifest.json.

skate_motion_kenney.json / skate_motion_kenney.gd contain authored animation
curves extracted from Kenney Mini Skate 2.0, also CC0. The real skate and
skate-stand clips provide torso/arm movement. They are not claimed as directly
compatible with the runner: the donor has single-bone legs. Legs, foot contact,
weight transfer, banking and mount/dismount transitions are procedural on the
existing Quaternius skeleton. The donor character is not used in the scene.
See skate_motion_manifest.json, docs/KENNEY_MINI_SKATE_LICENSE.txt and
docs/SKATE_ANIMATION.md. tools/prepare_skate_motion.py reproduces the extraction.
